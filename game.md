## Game Description

Weaponstrike Arena is a fast-paced shooting game inspired by Fortnite, set in a highly detailed New York City-style environment. The player explores a sprawling urban map, moves freely through the streets and rooftops, and engages enemy combatants in intense firefights.

### Key Features

- Fortnite-style shooter mechanics with free movement and aiming.
- Lush, high-fidelity visuals targeted for both mobile devices and desktop computers.
- New York City-inspired cityscape with skyscrapers, streets, alleys, and rooftop traversal.
- Player-controlled movement with walking, running, jumping, and shooting.
- Enemy AI that attacks from fixed positions and returns fire, while being limited in mobility.
- Cross-platform support for mobile and desktop input systems.

### Platform Goals

- Mobile: optimized controls, responsive performance, and polished graphics for modern phones and tablets.
- Desktop: keyboard/mouse support, high-resolution visuals, and smooth action gameplay.

### Gameplay Vision

Players should feel immersed in a detailed city arena, able to explore freely and engage enemies dynamically. The enemies should provide a challenge with ranged attacks, while the player enjoys superior movement freedom and fast-paced combat.
